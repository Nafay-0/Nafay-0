<h2>About</h2>

I'm Abdul Nafay Rizwani. I build language-model agents, then I ask a harder question: can you trust what their tools just told them?

I work at InChannel AI on Libbi Studio and Libbi Assist. I'm doing an MSc in Computer Science (software track) at the University of Helsinki. My thesis, *Verifying What Tools Tell Agents: Detecting Corrupted Tool Outputs in Language-Model Agents*, introduces **AgentGuard**, a checker that detects corrupted tool outputs in LLM agents.

<h2>Focus</h2>

- **LLM agents.** Tool-using models that have to hold up outside a demo.
- **Agent reliability.** Guardrails and output integrity, including a [guardrail fix](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/pull/50299) in [LiteLLM](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm).
- **AI products.** Libbi Studio and Libbi Assist at InChannel AI.

<h2>Featured</h2>

- **AgentGuard.** Master's thesis at the University of Helsinki. A checker that detects corrupted tool outputs in language-model agents.
- **LiteLLM.** Open-source contribution: a [guardrail fix](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/pull/50299) for Anthropic tool-result write-back.
- **[AI Comic Generation](https://github.com/Nafay-0/AI-Comic-Generation).** Comics generated with LLMs and Stable Diffusion.
